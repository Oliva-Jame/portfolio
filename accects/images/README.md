Personal Portfolio — Educational Researcher & Program Administrator
About

I am an educational researcher and program administrator dedicated to bridging data-driven insights with practical learning outcomes.

My work focuses on mixed-methods research, examining systemic educational challenges, educator resilience, and institutional development. I specialize in designing quantitative and qualitative frameworks—from field survey methodology and sampling to advanced statistical analysis in SPSS—to evaluate academic programs and inform evidence-based strategies.

Beyond data analysis, my work centers on making research methodologies and information systems accessible to educators and organizations. I regularly design training programs, develop manual frameworks, and explore knowledge management systems to streamline academic operations.

By combining practical research design with clear, actionable reporting, I aim to foster collaborative environments that strengthen educational access, program sustainability, and community-level impact.

Portfolio Goal

I want to develop my professional portfolio as a cloud-based personal website that brings together my research, educational work, program administration, training resources, and professional experience in one accessible digital space.

The portfolio will serve as a continuously developing platform where I can:

Present my professional background and areas of expertise
Showcase educational research projects
Document quantitative and qualitative research experience
Highlight SPSS and statistical analysis skills
Present training programs and educational resources
Share research publications and academic work
Showcase program administration experience
Organize professional documents and project materials
Build a long-term digital record of my work

Project Philosophy

Research should not remain only in data tables and reports. It should lead to practical knowledge, better decisions, and meaningful educational outcomes.

This portfolio reflects that philosophy by bringing together research, education, data, administration, and knowledge management in one digital space.
