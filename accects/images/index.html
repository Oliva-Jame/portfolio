About Me

Dedicated to fostering collaborative environments that strengthen educational access and program sustainability.

I am an educational researcher and program administrator dedicated to bridging data-driven insights with practical learning outcomes. My work focuses on mixed-methods research, examining systemic educational challenges, educator resilience, and institutional development.

I specialize in designing quantitative and qualitative frameworks — from field survey methodology and sampling to advanced statistical analysis in SPSS — to evaluate academic programs and inform evidence-based strategies.

Beyond data analysis, my work centers on making research methodologies and information systems accessible to educators and organizations. I regularly design training programs, develop manual frameworks, and explore knowledge management systems to streamline academic operations.

Education & Training

Training programs, teaching activities, workshops, and educational resources designed to build research capacity.

I am deeply committed to making research methodologies accessible to educators and emerging researchers. Through structured training programs, hands-on workshops, and carefully designed manuals, I help individuals and organizations build the skills needed for rigorous, meaningful research.

My training philosophy centers on practical application — turning abstract statistical concepts into tools that educators can use confidently in their own work. From introductory research methodology courses to advanced SPSS workshops, each program is designed with clear learning outcomes and real-world relevance.
